Welcome to SIES GST

SIES Graduate School of Technology (SIES GST) is a premier engineering institution located in Navi Mumbai,India. Established in 1998, SIES GST is part of the South Indian Education Society (SIES) group, which has a rich history of providing quality education in various fields. The institution offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering and technology, with a focus on fostering innovation, research, and industry collaboration. SIES GST is known for its state-of-the-art facilities, experienced faculty, and commitment to nurturing the talents of its students, preparing them for successful careers in the ever-evolving field of technology.
